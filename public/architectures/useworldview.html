01 / The system

An AI research engine with a direct data plane

Commands enter through the API. Long-running work moves through a durable Postgres queue. The browser reads study results directly from Supabase under row-level security.

CONTROL PLANE · API AUTHENTICATES AND SCHEDULESDATA PLANE · BROWSER READS ARE SCOPED BY RLS

The core pattern

Durable orchestration. A Postgres-backed job queue decouples user actions from long-running research and browser sessions.

The AI loop

Personas carry a worldview and situational context into an observe → decide → act cycle. Each step becomes a replayable interaction event.

The output

Individual sessions become findings, decision explanations, cross-persona patterns, and prioritized recommendations.
